How to play, in 3 taps
1
Pull back
Drag anywhere on the board. The dotted line shows where you'll go.
2
Hit the buttons
Let go to launch. Bounce off walls and pads to press every button.
3
Score!
The goal unlocks. Land the ball in it before you run out of launches.

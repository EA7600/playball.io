Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026 · applies to the Ball apps for iPhone and Mac and this website

The short version: Ball has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your progress stays on your device. The game only sends something over the internet in the few cases listed below, and never anything that tells us who you are, unless you choose to include your email address in a feedback message.

What stays on your device

Your levels, stars, coins, skins, backdrops, records, settings and the list of coin packs you have bought are saved on your device only. We can't see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

What the game sends, and why

  • Feedback you choose to send. If you write to us from Settings > Send feedback, your message, the email address you add (optional), your level, the game's language and your device type (for example "iPhone") are sent through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-to-email service, to our inbox. We use them only to read and answer your message and to improve the game. Ask us and we'll delete your message.
  • Players online. While the game is open it adds one to an anonymous counter for the current minute on Abacus (abacus.jasoncameron.dev), a public counting service. Nothing about you or your device is included; we only ever see a number, like "12 people played in the last minute".
  • News and gifts. The game downloads a small news file from our website, hosted on GitHub Pages, to show news and free gifts. Nothing about you is sent.
  • Purchases. Coin packs in the iPhone and Mac apps are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never receive your payment details or Apple ID. The app keeps each purchase's transaction number on your device so its coins are credited exactly once.

Like any website or online service, FormSubmit, Abacus, GitHub and Apple receive your device's IP address when the game connects to them, under their own privacy policies. We don't receive it.

This website

This website only tells you about Ball and links to the App Store; the game itself is in the apps. It has no forms, no cookies and no analytics. It remembers your language and light or dark choice in your browser, and loads its fonts from Google Fonts. GitHub, which hosts it, and Google receive your IP address when the pages load, under their own privacy policies.

What we don't do

  • No ads and no advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No account, sign-in, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access.

Children

Ball is made for everyone, including children. It doesn't ask for personal information. The only optional personal detail is an email address in a feedback message, so a younger player should ask a parent before adding one. If you believe a child sent us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

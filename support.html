Help with Ball, the one-tap physics puzzle

Something not working, or an idea for the game? Write to us. The quickest way is from inside the game: Settings > Send feedback. You can also email us:

ballplaynow@gmail.com

How do I play?

Drag back anywhere on the screen, then let go: Ball launches the opposite way. Bounce it into the goal using as few launches as you can to earn up to 3 stars. The "i" button in the game explains every piece as you meet it.

I bought coins but don't see them

Coins normally arrive right away. If the game closed during the purchase, open it again: Apple hands the purchase back and the coins are added then. If a purchase needs approval (Ask to Buy), the coins arrive once it's approved. For refunds, use Apple's Report a Problem page.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. There is no account, so deleting the app also deletes your progress and coins.

What are the Prize Machine's odds?

Inside the Prize Machine, tap the "i" button next to your coins to see the current drop rates. You only ever win things you don't have yet.

Can I change the language?

Yes: Settings > Language. Ball speaks English, Deutsch, Español, Français, Italiano, 日本語, 한국어, Português (Brasil), Русский and Türkçe.
